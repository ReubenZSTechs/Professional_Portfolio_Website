## The problem

Budgeting apps show charts, but they don't answer "can I afford this?" or explain where the money went. And most people would rather not send their bank statements to a hosted LLM. Tasya is the finance agent in [A.T.H.E.N.A](/projects/athena): budgeting, analysis and Q&A on your own data, running on local models.

## How it works

Tasya is built on the shared A.T.H.E.N.A workspace: a FastAPI + LangGraph backend, a Streamlit frontend, and training tools that fine-tune models with PEFT/LoRA, then merge and export them to GGUF for serving through Ollama. Answers combine **RAG** over the user's financial records and finance references with a model tuned using **RLHF**.
