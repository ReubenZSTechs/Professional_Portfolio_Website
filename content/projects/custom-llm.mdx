## The problem

Most of my work uses pretrained models through LangGraph, LoRA and RAG. To reason well about their failures and costs, I want to understand the layer underneath by building a small language model myself, end to end.

## How it works

The repository is laid out as a full training stack:

<Pipeline steps={["Raw text", "Preprocess + tokenizer", "Attention + transformer", "Train", "Evaluate / benchmark", "Inference API"]} highlight={2} />

- `configs/`: data, model and training configs kept separate from the code.
- `src/data`: loader, preprocessing and tokenizer.
- `src/model`: attention and the transformer architecture.
- `src/evaluation`: metrics and benchmarks; `src/inference`: generation and an API.
- `scripts/`: prepare data, train, evaluate and run inference; `notebooks/` for EDA.

<Callout>Early stage: the architecture and module layout are in place and the implementation is in progress.</Callout>
